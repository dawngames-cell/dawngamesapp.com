Website privacy

This website provides studio and project information without an account or a contact-form database. Email links open your email application; information you send by email is received by the studio.

The production website retains its existing GoatCounter page-visit analytics. It loads a script from gc.zgo.at and sends page-visit information to dawngamesapp.goatcounter.com. See GoatCounter’s privacy information for how the provider processes visits. The local review site does not load analytics.

Our site is served by its existing hosting provider. Visiting external Discord, store and privacy-policy links takes you to those providers, whose own policies apply. No new analytics or advertising scripts have been added to this redesign.

Published mobile-game privacy policy

The following is the existing game policy, retained separately from this website’s privacy information. Individual game pages also preserve their product-specific policies.

Last updated: April 7, 2026 · Dawn Games LLC

Overview

Dawn Games develops free-to-play mobile games (including Block Drop DG). This policy covers all games published by Dawn Games and explains what data is collected and how it's used.

Advertising

Our games use Google AdMob to display ads. AdMob may collect device advertising ID (GAID on Android, IDFA on iOS), IP address (anonymized), general location (country/region level), and app usage data for ad personalization.

iOS Users — App Tracking Transparency (ATT): On iOS, we will ask for your permission before tracking your activity across other companies' apps and websites for advertising purposes. You can change this at any time in your device settings under Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. If you decline tracking, you will still see ads, but they will be less personalized.

Android Users: You can opt out of personalized ads in your device settings under Google → Ads → Opt out of Ads Personalization.

Game Data

Game progress is stored locally on your device only. We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal information. No account creation or login required. No names, emails, or phone numbers collected.

Tournament Leaderboards

Some games (like Block Drop DG) feature weekly tournament leaderboards. If you participate, the following data is collected and stored on Google Firebase servers:

  • Display name (chosen by you in-game)
  • Score (game performance data)
  • Device identifier (to track unique players)
  • Timestamp (when score was submitted)

What is NOT collected: No email addresses, phone numbers, real names, location data, or any personally identifiable information.

Tournament scores are publicly visible on the leaderboard. You can opt out by not participating in tournaments.

In-App Purchases

Some of our games offer optional in-app purchases (e.g. virtual coins). All transactions are processed by Apple via StoreKit. We do not store or have access to your payment information. Purchases are non-refundable.

Analytics

We do not use any analytics or tracking SDKs beyond what is included in AdMob.

Children's Privacy

Our games are not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

Third-Party Services

  • • Google AdMob — Privacy Policy
  • • Google Firebase/Firestore — Privacy Policy
  • • Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) — Used to manage consent for ads and tracking.
  • • SKAdNetwork (iOS) — Apple's privacy-preserving ad attribution framework. No personal data is shared.

Data Retention

Local game data: Stored on your device only. Uninstalling deletes all local data.

Tournament data: Stored on Firebase servers for the duration of active tournaments (typically 7 days). Old tournament data is automatically deleted when new tournaments begin.

Questions? Contact us at: dawngames@dawngamesapp.com
