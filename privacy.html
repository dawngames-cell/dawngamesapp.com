Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 17, 2026 · Dawn Games

Overview

Dawn Games develops free-to-play mobile games. This policy covers all games published by Dawn Games and explains what data is collected and how it's used.

Advertising

Our games use Google AdMob to display ads. AdMob may collect device advertising ID (GAID on Android, IDFA on iOS), IP address (anonymized), general location (country/region level), and app usage data for ad personalization.

iOS Users — App Tracking Transparency (ATT): On iOS, we will ask for your permission before tracking your activity across other companies' apps and websites for advertising purposes. You can change this at any time in your device settings under Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. If you decline tracking, you will still see ads, but they will be less personalized.

Android Users: You can opt out of personalized ads in your device settings under Google → Ads → Opt out of Ads Personalization.

Game Data

Game progress is stored locally on your device only. We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal information. No account creation or login required. No names, emails, or phone numbers collected.

Analytics

We do not use any analytics or tracking SDKs beyond what is included in AdMob.

Children's Privacy

Our games are not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

Third-Party Services

Google AdMob — Google Privacy Policy

Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) — Used to manage consent for ads and tracking.

SKAdNetwork (iOS) — Apple's privacy-preserving ad attribution framework. No personal data is shared.

Data Retention

All game data is stored locally. Uninstalling any of our apps deletes all associated data.
