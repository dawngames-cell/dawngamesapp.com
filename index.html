About Dawn Games

INDEPENDENT
BY NATURE.
AMBITIOUS
BY DESIGN.

Small studio.
Room for a bigger world.

Dawn Games LLC is an independent game development studio focused on building immersive digital experiences with an emphasis on atmosphere, exploration and player freedom.

Our focus today is Dawn Upon Us. Our earlier games and digital projects are part of the journey that brought us here.

Meet the studio↗

Immersion

Worlds should feel believable. Atmosphere and detail make a place worth exploring.

Player freedom

Give players room to approach situations their way. Leave space for curiosity.

Iteration

Build, test, improve. Better experiences come from thoughtful work, one pass at a time.
