Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 19, 2026
Developer: Dawn Games LLC

Overview

Dawn Games develops free-to-play mobile games (including Darknet Idle and Hacker: Day in the Life). This policy covers all games published by Dawn Games and explains what data is collected and how it's used.

Advertising

Our games use Google AdMob to display ads. AdMob may collect device advertising ID, IP address (anonymized), general location (country/region level), and app usage data for ad personalization.

You can opt out of personalized ads in your device settings under Google → Ads → Opt out of Ads Personalization.

Game Data

Game progress is stored locally on your device only. We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal information. No account creation or login required. No names, emails, or phone numbers collected.

Analytics

We do not use any analytics or tracking SDKs beyond what is included in AdMob.

Children's Privacy

Our games are not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

Third-Party Services

Google AdMob — Google Privacy Policy

Data Retention

All game data is stored locally. Uninstalling any of our apps deletes all associated data.
