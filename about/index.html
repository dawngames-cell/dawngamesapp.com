About Dawn Games

An independent studio.
A deliberate next chapter.

Dawn Games LLC is focused on building immersive digital experiences with an emphasis on atmosphere, exploration and player freedom.

Our earlier mobile games and software projects gave us room to experiment. Today, Dawn Upon Us is our flagship: an open-world military experience in active development.

We’re building in the open, learning through iteration and sharing the work as it takes shape.
