Features Preview

Everything a scan tool does. Nothing you have to decode.

Four core tools, designed to be understood at a glance and safe to use on your own car.

Diagnose

Read the codes, in plain English

Pull fault codes and see them translated into plain language — what's wrong, what it affects, and what to check next. No cross-referencing a forum thread.

Maintenance

Track and reset in one tap

Keep an eye on service items — oil, brakes, filters, and more — and reset them the moment the work is done. No dealer visit to clear the reminder.

Coding

Customize with a safety net

Enable the features you want with guided coding. Every write is preceded by an automatic backup, so you can always roll back to how the car left the factory.

Live Data

Real numbers, in real time

Watch RPM, speed, coolant temp, throttle, boost, and battery voltage live as you drive — the vitals that tell you how the car is really doing.

Before You Start

What you'll need

Beamer Connect is still in development. Here's what it's being built to run on.

  • StatusIn development — early access is coming. Follow the build to know when it's ready.
  • DeviceiPhone running iOS 16 or later. iPhone-only at launch.
  • AdapterA WiFi OBD adapter (MHD or ELM327-compatible) plugged into your car's OBD-II port.
  • VehicleBMW. Feature and coding support varies by model and year.
