Development · Dawn Upon UsJune 21, 2026

Military POI, roads, and resistance hideouts

From the development archive. This entry describes an earlier build and is not a confirmation of current features or release plans.

Good build day. Finished the military POI — it's done and in place. Ran roads out from the big city so the world connects better instead of areas feeling isolated. Did a lot of terrain work too: new mountains, and smoothed out the older ones that were looking rough. Also started building the Dawn resistance areas — hidden spots where survivors lie low and stay out of the Watch's sight. The world's starting to feel like a place with two sides actually living in it.
