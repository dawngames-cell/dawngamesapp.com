Privacy Policy

Effective date: March 23, 2026
Developer: Dawn Games LLC

1. Information We Collect

Block Drop DG collects minimal data to provide and improve the game experience. Game progress and scores are stored locally on your device. We use Google AdMob to display ads, which may collect device identifiers, IP address, and usage data to serve ads.

2. How We Use Information

To save your game progress locally, to display advertisements via Google AdMob, to process in-app purchases, and to maintain leaderboard functionality.

3. In-App Purchases

Block Drop DG offers optional in-app purchases (virtual coins). All transactions are processed by Apple via StoreKit. We do not store or have access to your payment information. Purchases are non-refundable. Coins are added instantly.

4. Third-Party Services

Google AdMob (advertising) — Google Privacy Policy
Apple StoreKit (in-app purchases) — Apple Privacy Policy

5. Data Storage

Game data (scores, settings, purchases) is stored locally on your device. Leaderboard scores may be stored on our servers with only a display name and score value. Uninstalling the app deletes all local data.

6. Children's Privacy

Block Drop DG does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

7. Your Choices

You can reset your game data by deleting and reinstalling the app. You can limit ad tracking in your device settings (Settings > Privacy > Tracking).

8. Contact

For questions about this privacy policy, contact: dawngames@dawngamesapp.com
