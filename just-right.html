Information We Collect

Personal Information
We do NOT collect any personally identifiable information. Just Right can be played completely anonymously.

Non-Personal Information
We may collect the following non-personal information:

  • Game Statistics: High scores, coins earned, themes unlocked (stored locally on your device only)
